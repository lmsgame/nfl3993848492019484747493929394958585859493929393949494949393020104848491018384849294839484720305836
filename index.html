How to Play

  1. Draft 8 legends — one for each key position (QB, RB, WR, TE, OL, EDGE, LB, DB).
  2. Each round shows 3 random all-time greats. Pick your favorite.
  3. You have 2 rerolls total. Use them wisely.
  4. After the draft, the game simulates all 17 games based on your roster strength.
  5. Higher ratings + chemistry = better shot at going undefeated.
  6. Goal: 17-0. Join the 1972 Dolphins in history.
